# Syllabus & Course Policies

## Grading Breakdown
Grading will be based on weekly quizzes (**24%**), homeworks (**50%**), a course project (**25%**), and attendance (**1%**).

* **11-485 Base Score:** Assignments (50%) + Quiz (24%) + Attendance (1%) = 75%
* **11-685 / 11-785 Base Score:** 11-485 Base Score (75%) + Project (25%) = 100%

---

## Quizzes (24%)
* There will be weekly quizzes.
* We retain your best **12 out of 14** quizzes taken.
* Quizzes are generally released on Fridays and due 48 hours later, on Sundays.
* Quizzes account for **24%** of your overall score.

---

## Homeworks (50%)
There are four homework assignments in total (HW1 through HW4). Each homework (other than HW4) consists of two parts:
* **HWxP1:** Low-level operations implemented in a deterministic setting. Evaluated on-time based on correctly completed test parts submitted to Gradescope.
* **HWxP2:** Open-ended Kaggle competition. Evaluated based on model performance on test benchmarks.
  * **Early Submission Deadline:** Submit at least one valid submission by this deadline to receive a **3% bonus**.
  * **On-time Deadline:** Standard deadline for HWxP2.
  * **Slack Days:** Each student receives **10 slack days** for the semester to distribute across HWxP2 only.
  * **Kaggle Scoring Cutoffs:** Benchmarked at HIGH (90%), MEDIUM (70%), LOW (50%), and VERY LOW (30%).
* **Submission Portals:** Releases on **Canvas**, submissions on **Gradescope** and **Autolab**.

---

## Course Project (25%)
All students enrolled in the graduate versions (11-785 / 11-685) are required to complete a comprehensive deep learning project:
* **Midterm Report:** 20%
* **Project Video:** 35%
* **Piazza Interaction:** 5%
* **Final Project Report:** 40%
* **Peer Review:** Every student reviews 4–6 peer project videos after classes finish.

---

## Attendance (1%)
* **Section A:** In-person attendance is expected and tracked.
* **Remote / Out-of-Timezone Sections:** Must attend live via Zoom or watch on **MediaServices** before Monday 8:00 AM EST of the following week.
* Credit is granted to students attending at least **70%** of randomly audited lectures.

---

## Academic Integrity
Students are expected to strictly comply with the **CMU University Policy on Academic Integrity and Plagiarism**. You may discuss high-level concepts and ideas with peers, but **all code and written reports must be your own work**. Sharing code or using unauthorized assistance will result in severe course penalties up to failure and university disciplinary action.
