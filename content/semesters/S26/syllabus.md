# Syllabus & Course Policies (Spring 2026)

## Policy Breakdown

### Grading Breakdown
Grading will be based on weekly quizzes (**24%**), homeworks (**50%**), and a course project (**25%**). Note that **1%** of your grade is assigned to Attendance.

* **11-485 Base Score:** Assignments (50%) + Quiz (24%) + Attendance (1%) = **75%** (scaled to 100%)
* **11-685 / 11-785 Base Score:** Assignments (50%) + Quiz (24%) + Attendance (1%) + Project (25%) = **100%**
* **Bonus Points:** Up to 5% total bonus points contribute directly on top of the base score.

---

### Quizzes (24%)
There will be weekly quizzes throughout the semester:
* We will retain your **best 12 out of 14** quizzes.
* Quizzes will generally (but not always) be released on Friday and due 48 hours later.
* Quizzes are scored by the number of correct answers.
* **Quizzes are worth 24% of your overall score.**

---

### Assignments (50%)
There will be four assignments in all, plus the Peer Review assignment during the last week of the semester. Assignments include an **Autolab** component (implementing low-level operations from scratch) and a **Kaggle** component (competing on competitive Deep Learning tasks).

* **Autolab:** Scored according to the number of correctly completed test cases and parts.
* **Kaggle Performance Cutoffs:** Performance cutoffs are established for **HIGH (90%)**, **MEDIUM (70%)**, **LOW (50%)**, and **VERY LOW (30%)**. Scores are linearly interpolated between cutoffs.
* **Deadlines per Assignment:**
  * **Early Submission Deadline:** You must make at least one submission to Kaggle by this deadline. Missing this deadline incurs a 10% penalty on subsequent homework marks.
  * **On-Time Deadline:** Submissions by this deadline are eligible for up to 5 bonus points based on interpolation up to 105.
  * **Late Deadline:** Submissions after the on-time deadline incur a 10% penalty.
  * **Slack Days:** Every student receives **10 slack days** across all homework Part 2s only. Once slack days are exhausted, late penalties apply by default.
  * **Kaggle Scoring Formula:** $\max(\text{on-time score}, \text{slack-day score}, 0.9 \times \text{late-submission score})$.
* **Peer Review Assignment:** Required for all students (11-485/685/785). Students review and grade 4–6 project videos over the final weekend (15–20 minutes per review), leaving meaningful comments and feedback.

---

### Course Project (25%)
All graduate students (11-785 and 11-685) are required to complete a course project worth **25%** of the overall grade:
* **Midterm Report:** 20%
* **Project Video Presentation:** 35%
* **Piazza Feedback & Responses:** 5%
* **Final Project Report:** 40%
* Important rubrics and video timelines are detailed in the [Project Evaluation Guidelines](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nguV-Z45QJkQUambJ4dv-SijA_ttsDQnwUYlbBOzicw/edit#gid=0).

---

### Attendance (1%)
* **Section A (In-Person):** Expected to attend lectures in person (Giant Eagle Auditorium, Baker Hall A51). Attendance is tracked.
* **Remote / Inconvenient Timezone Sections:** Must attend live on Zoom or watch recordings on [MediaServices](https://mediaservices.cmu.edu/channel/Introduction+to+Deep+Learning_Spring+2026/397642153) before **Monday 8:00 AM EST** of the following week.
* Attending at least **70%** of randomly audited lectures awards the full 1% attendance point.

---

### Grading Policies & Standards
* **Curving:** The end-of-term grade is curved based on relative class performance.
* **Pass/Fail:** Students registered for P/F must complete all quizzes, homeworks, and the project (graduate sections). A grade equivalent to **B-** is required to pass.
* **Auditing:** Auditors must complete all quizzes and homeworks; the project is strongly encouraged but optional.

---

### Collaboration, Study Groups & Integrity
* **Study Groups:** Collaboration in study groups for quizzes and homework conceptual ablations is highly encouraged.
* **Academic Integrity:** All students must strictly comply with the [CMU Policy on Academic Integrity](https://www.cmu.edu/policies/student-and-student-life/academic-integrity.html). You may discuss ideas and concepts with peers, but **sharing code is strictly forbidden**. All submitted code must be your own original work.
